# Catalog refresh — 2026-10-12

Direct review of official provider sources refreshed 15 model records and 14
active price records and removed one deprecated identity. This covers the 12
model and 14 price records whose September 9 evidence dates exceeded the release
gate's 30-day limit, plus four Vertex model records reviewed alongside their
prices. The catalog and price book now contain 36 identities. This was a targeted
review, not an automated sweep.

## Azure

Reviewed GPT-5.4 Mini/Nano, GPT-5.5, and GPT-5.6 Luna/Sol/Terra against
[Azure's model table](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/foundry-models/concepts/models-sold-directly-by-azure)
and [prompt-caching documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/how-to/prompt-caching).
Model identities, limits, modalities, capabilities, and release dates agree with
the catalog. Month-only training cutoffs remain unspecified.

Queried the [Azure Retail Prices API](https://prices.azure.com/api/retail/prices)
for product `Azure OpenAI GPT5`, region `eastus`, and meter names containing
`5.4`, `5.5`, or `5.6`. Compared USD `Gl` meters for global Standard inference,
plus global Batch meters for Mini/Nano; priority and data-zone meters were
excluded. All represented rates agree except GPT-5.6 Sol:

| Context | Input | Output | Cache read | Cache write |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Up to 272,000 tokens | 4 | 20 | 0.4 | 5 |
| Above 272,000 tokens | 8 | 30 | 0.8 | 10 |

Rates are USD per million tokens. The retail meters report September 1 as their
effective start. The corrected local schedule begins on this review date,
preserving previously recorded cost estimates. Tiered Batch rates remain omitted
because the schema cannot express them.
