GPT-6 prompts above 272,000 input tokens double input/cache rates and multiply
output rates by 1.5 for the entire request. Tier-specific cache-write prices are
included. Batch pricing also has context tiers, which the existing single-tier
Batch field cannot represent; it stays null rather than publishing an incorrect
flat rate. Regional and Fast/Flex variants are not represented by these base prices.
Release dates come from the [OpenAI changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog).

Anthropic entries include five-minute and one-hour cache writes and Batch
input/output prices. Structured-output support is confirmed by the
[compatibility list](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs).
Month-only knowledge cutoffs remain null, and minimum retirement commitments are
not converted into exact retirement dates. Model aliases and dated prefixes are
not invented. Cross-provider deployments require their own pricing evidence.

Gemini 3.8 Flash has separate schedules through December 31, 2026 and from
January 1, 2027. Its Standard input/output rates change from 0.75/3.75 to 1.50/7.50;
cache and Batch rates change on the same date. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite includes
Standard and Batch rates. Google's time-based cache-storage charges remain outside
the token-price schema.

Sonnet 4.6, Sonnet 5, GPT-5.6 Luna, and GPT-5.6 Terra. Their numeric Standard rates

confirms previously missing Batch cached-input prices: 0.0125 for
`gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (text/image/video) and 0.075 for `gemini-3.5-flash`.
The former model's separate audio-input rate is not represented by the existing
modality-independent token schema.
